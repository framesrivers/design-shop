Questions

What kinds of websites do you build?

I design and build service, portfolio, small-business, and campaign websites. We choose the platform and scope around what you need to manage, how quickly you need to launch, and where the site should grow next.

Can you handle branding and UX/UI together?

Yes. A project can combine brand direction, website strategy, UX/UI, and development so everything feels connected instead of pieced together.

What does monthly support include?

Monthly support can cover website updates, social graphics, brand assets, UX/UI improvements, launch help, and hosting care. We agree on priorities and a realistic amount of work each month.

Can you maintain my social media?

Yes. I can help with ongoing graphics, profile and highlight updates, content organization, and scheduling-ready posts. We can shape the exact mix around the channels you use.

Can you manage hosting and domains?

Yes. I can set up hosting, connect domains, manage updates and backups, and troubleshoot common website issues. You keep ownership of every account and approve outside costs first.

What does a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope, timeline, and the level of strategy or ongoing support involved. Send a short note about what you need and I'll recommend a clear next step before any work begins.

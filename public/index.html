Questions

Can I edit a Canva website myself?

Yes. A Canva website template opens in your Canva account so you can edit the content and publish your version. You'll need a Canva account; some publishing options require a paid plan.

How will Squarespace templates work?

I'll build the editable site in Squarespace. After purchase, I can make you a copy and transfer it to your Squarespace account. You'll choose and pay for your own Squarespace site plan.

What do I get with an HTML template?

Website files you can upload to your own hosting account. You can edit the files yourself or ask about installation help.

When can I buy a template?

The first products are being made now. Send me a note if you'd like to hear when a specific collection is ready.

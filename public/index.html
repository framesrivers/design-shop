Questions

What kinds of websites do you build?

Sites for service businesses, salons, bakeries, shops, portfolios and launches. We pick the platform based on what you want to update yourself and how fast you need to go live.

¿Trabajas en español?

¡Sí! Podemos hacer todo el proyecto en español, desde el primer mensaje hasta el lanzamiento y el soporte mensual.

Can you do my branding and website together?

Yes, and it's the best way to do it. Your logo, colors and site get designed side by side, so nothing looks stitched together.

What does monthly support include?

Whatever your business needs that month: site updates, social graphics, new brand pieces, design fixes or hosting help. We agree on a realistic amount of work up front.

Can you manage hosting and domains?

Yes. I set up hosting, connect domains, handle updates and backups, and fix things when they break. You keep ownership of every account and approve any outside costs first.

What does a project cost?

It depends on what you need. See my plans, or send me a quick note and I'll come back with a clear plan and price before any work starts.

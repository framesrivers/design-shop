Questions we
hear a lot

Don't see yours? Call or message us — we answer in English or Spanish.

Talk to Andrés or Lucía(956) 555-0173
Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. Our whole team works in English and Spanish, from the first call to closing. Texas contracts are in English, but we'll walk you through every line in whichever language you prefer.

What's in the free staging consultation?

With every listing, Lucía tours your home and gives you a room-by-room plan: what to move, what to store and which fixes are worth it. Then she shoots the listing photos herself.

Can you help me buy a ranch or acreage?

It's one of our specialties. Andrés spent ten years in construction, so he looks over fencing, wells, septic, access roads and outbuildings with a builder's eye before you make an offer.

I'm relocating for a medical or teaching job.

We regularly help doctors, nurses, teachers and university staff moving to the Valley. We can tour homes with you by video, build a shortlist around your shifts and commute, and line things up before you arrive.

Do you work with investors?

Yes. We help you find single-family rentals and duplexes in McAllen, Mission and Edinburg, and check the numbers — expected rent, repairs and carrying costs — before you buy.

What are your office hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9am–7pm. Sundays by appointment. You'll find us at 2900 N Ware Rd, Suite 105, in McAllen.
